SHAKIB SOFTWARE ENGINEER PORTFOLIO WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html: website content
- style.css: visual design and responsive layout
- script.js: mobile menu, live Dhaka clock, map selector, contact email draft
- assets/profile.png: your profile photo

HOW TO OPEN:
1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Open the SHakib_Portfolio_With_Profile folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open the website in your browser.

BEFORE PUBLISHING:
- Replace 'Add your email address' in index.html with your real email address.
- Replace sample project cards with your actual projects.
- The map requires an internet connection.
- The contact form opens the visitor's default email app; it does not send through a server.
